Rules (POS; voucher-entry screens reuse 1, 4, 5, 6).
  1. Two columns: left (fill) = "Người mua" card on top + "Mặt hàng" card filling the rest; right = "Thanh toán" card fixed at 340 px, full height. 16 px gaps. The voucher's number and date live in the header subtitle.
  2. Buyer type is a segmented control: RadioButtons with Appearance = Button, FlatStyle Flat; checked = accent fill. Types 1–2 show the detainee picker + card and pay "Trừ lưu ký"; types 3–4 show a free-text buyer, pay cash, and the balance rows are hidden.
  3. Detainee card: initials tile, name semibold, meta line muted, status dot on the right. Focus jumps to the item lookup after a pick.
  4. Item lookup: a 40 px InputFrame (11pt). The suggestion dropdown shows mã, tên, ĐVT, giá, tồn; out-of-stock items are greyed with a red "Hết hàng" and refused. Enter adds 1 and clears the box; scanning the same code again adds 1.
  5. Only Số lượng is editable (F2 or typing). The cell being edited has a 2 px accent outline.
  6. Thanh toán: amounts 14pt semibold, right-aligned. "Số dư sau khi mua" green when ≥ 0. Total box --accent-soft with a 24pt bold amount and the amount in words below. A primary 44 px "Ghi sổ F9" button. No AcceptButton on this form: Enter belongs to the lookup [ASSUMPTION F9].

Rules. The client never lets a cart exceed the balance: the shortfall row replaces "Số dư sau", the total box turns --danger-soft, a red banner explains, and Ghi sổ is disabled. The service still re-checks under row lock; if the balance changed meanwhile, the same text appears as a MessageBox (Warning) and nothing is posted. An out-of-stock line at posting turns its grid row red and the message names it.

Rules. One confirmation dialog layout for every posting in the app: an icon tile for the voucher type, a 17px question, the subject in muted text, a before → amount → after table, the amount in words, and a footer with the print option on the left and the buttons on the right (primary last). AcceptButton = Ghi sổ, Esc = Quay lại. The print checkbox remembers its last value per workstation [ASSUMPTION]. After posting, the screen clears for the next buyer and focus returns to Đối tượng.
